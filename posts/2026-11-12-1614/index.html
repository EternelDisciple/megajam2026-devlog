Day 1, late afternoon: a style is a sum of refusals

In short: every tool we use is public. What makes the look ours is what we keep, and what we throw away.

Turned down today

  • Pixel art cards in a classic fantasy frame: too close to a 1990s trading card.
  • Recoloured public-domain engravings: beautiful, but a collage of other people's scenes.
  • Colour that grows with rarity: busy, and it buried the line work.

Kept

  • Our own designs, drawn as engravings with a light wash. The closest we have been to the game in our heads.
  • A modern card face: the cost reads exactly like the energy gauge.
  • Rarity carried by material and light. Most cards stay matte, so the rare ones shine.

Our rule

We show what we chose and why. The exact how stays in the workshop.
