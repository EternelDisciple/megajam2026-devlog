# One card, one technique, and it grows with use

![In game: a technique reaches level 2 mid-fight against the Minotaur](media/2026-11-13-1310/2026-11-13_1300_level_up_ingame.jpg "Playtest build, in French.")

**In short**: we merged our cards into techniques. Rarity changes what a technique does; using it well makes it stronger.

### What changed
- One card is one technique with one name, in four rarities.
- Rarity changes the shape on the grid and adds an effect.
- Each copy earns experience only when it succeeds: it hits, applies its status, or blocks a real blow.
- Three levels, shown on the card's edge. Level up pops right after the damage numbers.

### Decisions
- The deck you start with has no element. Your style comes during the run.
- A forge fuses two identical copies into the next rarity. You lose both, and keep half their experience.
