# Two allies in the tower

![The Spirit and the Smith](media/2026-11-14-0318/2026-11-13_1400_allies.jpg "Simulated game render.")

**In short**: between fights, two characters keep you company. One gives you cards, the other fuses them.

### The Spirit
- Greets you at the start of each run, and again after each death: "Ah, there you are again."
- Hands you your first cards, and sells more when you find it again.
- Lavender and ivory, a halo, cards orbiting around it.

### The Smith
- Two identical cards on the anvil, a few hammer blows, one stronger card comes out.
- Ember and leather, a card heated red held in her tongs.

Both are built with the same tools and level of detail as our bosses.
