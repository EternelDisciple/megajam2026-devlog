# We turned the heavy lighting back on

![Two small environments, a nave and a snowy terrace: raw render above, in-game pixel filter below](media/2026-11-13-0621/2026-11-13_0617_vignettes_moteur_en.jpg "Work in progress. Top row of each set: raw render. Bottom row: our current pixel filter.")

**In short**: two tiny scenes to validate the whole set pipeline before the 7 arenas.

### What we built
- A gothic architecture kit, scripted by us: 28 pieces.
- Two final-look vignettes of a few square metres: an indoor nave and a terrace under snow.
- Dynamic global illumination and many lights are back. We're here for the tour de force.

### What the test showed
Raw, the nave works: cold moon shaft, warm braziers. Through our pixel filter it loses a notch: darks crush, and lit stone turns to noise.

### Decision
We write our own pixel shader, v2. It pixelates at the source instead of averaging afterwards.

**Update (Nov 14):** We later dropped Lumen after measuring its cost; many dynamic lights remain, through MegaLights. Temporal anti-aliasing, not the pixel filter, turned out to be the main source of flicker.
