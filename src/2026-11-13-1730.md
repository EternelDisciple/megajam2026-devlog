# The Hydra

![Six heads, each one waiting for its turn](media/2026-11-13-1730/2026-11-13_1558_hydra_six_heads.gif "Simulated game render.")

**In short**: our new boss, built around one old myth. Cut a head, two grow back.

### How it fights
- One head, one attack per turn. More heads means more pressure.
- Each head will have its own small health bar.
- Cut one and two grow back the next turn.
- Unless the neck is burning: fire seals the wound, like Heracles did.
- You win by bringing down the body, not by cutting every head.

### Why
It rewards the fire path and punishes waiting. The longer the fight, the more heads.
