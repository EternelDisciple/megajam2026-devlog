# Six signs on the doors

![The six door signs](media/2026-11-14-0918/2026-11-14_0814_door_signs.png "Final icons, shown at 64 px and enlarged.")

**In short**: each door now tells you what waits behind it with one small sign, no text.

### The six signs
- **Health**: the Heart of the Tower, a stone heart cracked with red light.
- **Card**: a soul set on a card.
- **Gold**: a stack of coins.
- **Action point**: a glowing ember.
- **Forge**: anvil and hammer.
- **The Spirit**: our ghostly ally.

### What we kept
- 64 pixels each, shown as they are, with crisp pixels.
- Thick shapes that read at a glance, one colour per reward.

### What we dropped
- A cup for health: nobody read it as "health".
- Giant medallions: the door must stay the subject, so the sign is now small.
