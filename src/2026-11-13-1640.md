# Bosses that think, and a new one with six heads

![Bosses now pick their attack from the situation](media/2026-11-13-1640/2026-11-13_1628_boss_ai.jpg "Playtest build.")

**In short**: every boss now reads the board before it attacks. Our test bot went from winning every run to winning 70%.

### What changed
- Each boss has its own rules: lined up with it, at its side, or hidden behind a pillar, you get a different attack.
- It thinks the same way at every stage. Later stages only add new tools.
- At the highest stage, it acts once without warning, by a fixed rule you learn the hard way.
- One attack in two is too wide to just step out of. A pillar, a guard or the right card saves you.

### New boss
The Hydra replaces the Legion. One head, one attack. Cut a head and two grow back, unless the neck burns.
