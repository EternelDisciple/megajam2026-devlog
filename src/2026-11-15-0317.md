# A room pass in 23 minutes

![The Vampire's ballroom before and after the art pass](media/2026-11-15-0317/2026-11-15_0253_vampire_room_pass.jpg "Same layout, same turn. Playtest build.")

**In short**: the Minotaur's lair looked far better than the other boss rooms. We applied the same recipe to the Vampire's ballroom and timed it.

### What changed
- Generic props replaced by props modelled for pixel scale: ballroom columns, coffins in the alcoves, a fallen chandelier, candelabra.
- One cold key light instead of three fighting each other.
- A dance floor engraved in the stone, so the room is recognisable at a glance.
- The layout did not move: same tiles, same cover, same fight.

### What it cost
- 23 minutes, 6 of them waiting for the build lock.
- Most of it was reuse: the modelling scripts, room template and offscreen checks built for the Minotaur.
- Next: the six remaining rooms, one after another.
