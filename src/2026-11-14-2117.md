# A hand that feels right

![Sweeping the mouse across the hand](media/2026-11-14-2117/2026-11-14_2020_hand_hover.gif "Hand hover after the rework. Playtest build.")

**In short**: while playtesting, the hand felt wrong. Cards jumped, and picking one was tiring. We rebuilt how the hover works.

### What was wrong
- The enlarged card decided what was under the mouse, so it held on, then let go all at once.

### What we kept
- Each card owns a fixed zone, based on where it rests.
- When the hovered card changes, it settles where the mouse was at that moment, then stays put.
- Neighbours slide aside on springs.
- Tested on hands of 5, 7 and 9 cards: every card activates once, in order.

### Also
- Cards now breathe: name on top, centred text, keywords explained on hover.
