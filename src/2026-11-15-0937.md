# Every card now hits like it means it

![Fire blades, common then legendary](media/2026-11-15-0937/2026-11-15_0719_flame_blades.gif "Ember, common then legendary, against the Minotaur. Playtest build.")

**In short**: every attack used to play the same generic hit. Now each technique has its own shape, and rarer cards hit harder on screen.

### What changed
- Fire bursts out of the floor as blades, tile by tile, then one big blade lands on the target.
- Every technique follows the same rule: steel crescents, thrusts, ice spikes, rock pillars, falling blocks.
- Damage lands when the effect reaches the boss, not before.

### Rarity you can feel
- Same card, four rarities: a wider arc, a broader wave, then extra rings, a gold flash and a stronger shake.
- We shrank the legendary gold wave: it was hiding the boss's warning tiles.
