# The door hall, rebuilt

![Before and after: the hall of three doors](media/2026-11-13-1525/2026-11-13_1427_door_hall.jpg "Before: greybox arches. After: the nave kit, with each boss on its door.")

**In short**: the room where you pick your next boss now matches the rest of the game.

### What changed
- Built from our own nave kit: three doors over eleven metres tall, a tiny hero, steps that climb.
- Each door shows its boss, its stage, and what waits behind it: a card, gold, the forge, the ally.
- Relics stay secret. You find out after the fight.
- Heavier light: cold moon, two braziers, a lavender glow at the thresholds.

### Next
