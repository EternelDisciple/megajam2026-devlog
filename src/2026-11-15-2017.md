# Three looks in four days: our interface

![The Spirit's card pick, before and after: crisp pixel frames on the left, dark illumination on the right](media/2026-11-15-2017/2026-11-15_cheminement_ui_avant_apres_en.jpg "Same screen, one day apart. Playtest build.")

**In short**: we expected one interface style to last. It took three.

### The path
- Day 1: ink and paper, then square and plain. Readable, but charmless.
- Day 2: crisp pixel frames, to match the world.
- Day 4: the layout was right, the material was not. It still read like a debug menu.

### What we did
We kept every screen where it was and changed only its skin: bevelled metal, name ribbons, a faint vellum grain.

### Why
Pixels suit the characters. Interface frames needed more character than pixels gave.
