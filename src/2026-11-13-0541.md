# The hero's sword went through his arm

![The knight crouching in a horizontal slash, sword cleanly held](media/2026-11-13-0541/2026-11-13_0341_heros_cast_iai.gif "Work in progress: the new crouched horizontal slash.")

**In short**: we measured the grip instead of trusting the check.

### The problem
- On every frame, the weapon crossed the shoulder and forearm.
- An automatic check was green anyway: it never tested the handle.

### The fix, at the source
- New check rule: the weapon must stay out of the body.
- Grip rebuilt, wrist range limited.
- All 19 animation clips pass.

![Before and after close-ups of the sword grip](media/2026-11-13-0541/2026-11-13_0341_heros_prise_epee_en.png "Before / after, from several angles.")

### New
A crouched horizontal slash replaces the old cast pose.

We fixed the rule, not just the frames: a green check that misses the bug is worse than no check.
