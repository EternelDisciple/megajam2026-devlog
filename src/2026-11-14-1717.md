# One pixel grid, one reference arena

![The eight arenas of a run, built from one template](media/2026-11-14-1717/2026-11-14_1654_eight_arenas.jpg "Same floor, same light; only the boss glow changes. Playtest build.")

**In short**: last post's open question is answered. The scenery no longer shimmers, and all arenas now come from one reference.

### What changed
- Walls and floor now sit on the same pixel grid as characters, pinned to the world.
- When the camera follows the hero, 0.6 % of pixels change, down from 6.8 %.
- We polished one arena, then a script writes it into the seven others.
- Each boss changes a single thing: the colour of its glow.

### What we dropped
- A softer, blurrier scenery filter: it read as out of focus.

### Still open
- The floor is all blue. Material variety comes once the layout is final.
