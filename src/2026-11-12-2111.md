# Day 1, late night: the tower fills up

![The hero next to the eight act-one antagonists, all playing the same action at once](media/2026-11-12-2111/2026-11-12_2111_bestiary.gif "Hero top left, then the Minotaur, Fenrir, the Lich, Medusa, the Vampire, the Legion, the Inquisitor and the Siren.")

**In short**: act I now has its whole cast, each one a vice made flesh.

### The cast
- The Minotaur, Wrath: huge, charges in straight lines, dazed by walls.
- The Lich, Attachment: keeps coming back while her phylactery stands.
- Medusa, Shame: hides her face until she looks at you.
- Fenrir, Hunger: chained, eats your cards, grows.
- The Vampire, Dependence: drains, then melts into mist.
- The Legion, Rumination: splits into copies of himself.
- The Inquisitor, Judgement: bans a type of card for two turns.
- The Siren, Oblivion: the final fight, and the song from the menu.
