# One room per boss

![The 8 rooms on the hero's first turn](media/2026-11-14-2317/2026-11-14_2218_rooms_per_boss.jpg "The 8 boss rooms, hero's turn 1. Playtest build.")

**In short**: every boss now fights in a room of its own. Same walls, same light, but a layout built around how that boss plays.

### What changed
- One closed room, wall to wall: every tile is playable, no more empty border.
- Each boss gets its own layout: pylons for the Minotaur, a fire pit for Fenrir, fords for the Hydra, a chasm for the Lich, water for the Siren.
- Rooms are bigger (15 to 17 tiles wide), so the camera pulls back a little.

### How we checked it
- Our bot played all 8 rooms: no fight got stuck.
