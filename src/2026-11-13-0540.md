# Sixty cards overnight, then a change of course

![All 60 card illustrations of the first version, on a single sheet](media/2026-11-13-0540/2026-11-13_0410_cartes60_en.jpg "Version 1: the 60 cards, done in one night. Work in progress.")

**In short**: we made every card illustration overnight, then chose to redo them.

### What happened
- All 60 illustrations came out of one 16-minute GPU pass.
- A rarity test showed the problem: the frame tells the tier, but not the power. Power was hard to read at hand size.

### Decision
Illustrations will become captures of staged scenes inside the engine, with the same pixel grain as the board.
- They regenerate from card data, so design changes cost nothing.
- Real effects grow with rarity.
- Version 1 stays as our safety net until the new cards beat it.

We dropped the quick route because consistency with the board matters more than speed.
