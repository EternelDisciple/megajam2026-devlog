# Every boss makes an entrance

![Two signed entrances: the Minotaur and Fenrir](media/2026-11-13-2118/2026-11-13_1908_boss_entrances.mp4 "Long version on first meeting, short version after.")

**In short**: bosses no longer just drop in. Each one gets a short entrance of its own.

### What it does
- The Minotaur falls from the ceiling, lands knees bent, and the ground shakes.
- Fenrir starts in the dark: two red eyes, then a slow light on his shape.
- About three seconds the first time, under two after. One click skips it.
- No full-screen flash, and calmer camera when reduced motion is on.

### Also in
The doors now show each boss as a stone bust, and below it a small statue of what waits behind.
