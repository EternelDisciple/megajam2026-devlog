# One deck, action points and movement points

![In combat: 3 AP as capsules, 3 MP as dots, one deck](media/2026-11-13-1530/2026-11-13_1514_ap_mp.jpg "Playtest build.")

**In short**: we dropped our second deck. Moving no longer costs a card.

### The decision
- Before: a movement deck and an action deck, two draw piles to read.
- Now: one deck, plus action points and movement points every turn, like many tactics games.
- Easier to read in combat, and no turn without a way to move.
- Special moves (step over, dashes, leaps) stay as cards in the deck.

### Also in
- After each boss, pick one relic out of three. Relics change how you play.
- Three of them came almost for free: +1 AP, +1 MP, +1 card per turn.
