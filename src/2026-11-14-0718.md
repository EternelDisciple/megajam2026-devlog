# One card out of three

![The Spirit offers three cards](media/2026-11-14-0718/2026-11-14_0701_one_of_three.jpg "Playtest build.")

**In short**: the Spirit's gift is now a real choice.

### Before
- Five cards fanned out on screen.
- Keep none, some or all of them.
- In practice, you took almost everything, and nothing felt like a decision.

### Now
- Three cards in a straight row, at the same height.
- One per path: Fire, Ice, Stone.
- Take one, or skip.

From the very first room, you choose a direction for your deck. The same rule now applies to every geode: after a fight, at the merchant, after a death.

We checked it in our simulated runs: the win rate stays in our target range, with no change to the starting deck.
