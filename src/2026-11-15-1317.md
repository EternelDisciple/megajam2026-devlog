# The first shot shows what's at stake

![From the three doors down to the knight](media/2026-11-15-1317/2026-11-15_1305_hub_arrival_doors.gif "First arrival in the hub: the three doors light up, then the camera glides down to the knight. Sped up. Playtest build.")

**In short**: the first time you reach the hub, the camera now opens on the three doors, then glides down to the knight climbing the stairs.

### Why
- The old version zoomed far out to show the whole hall. It showed a lot, and it showed the empty, unfinished edges too.
- The doors are the whole game: pick one boss, the other two grow stronger. So they come first, lit one by one.

### Also fixed
- The knight used to slide through the staircase like a ghost. He now walks up each step.
- The Spirit no longer talks to you from across the room: you walk up to it.
