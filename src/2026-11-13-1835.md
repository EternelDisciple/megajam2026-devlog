# 57 cards, shot inside the game

![The starting hand: each card shows its own technique and its pattern](media/2026-11-13-1835/2026-11-13_1747_hand.jpg "Playtest build, in French.")

**In short**: every card illustration is now a frame captured in the game itself, with the real hero, the real effects and the real pixel filter.

### What changed
- One staging per technique: its own camera angle and its own room.
- The same shot across the four rarities, so you see the technique grow.
- The pattern is drawn on the card, next to the cost.
- No more shared placeholder art: you know what a card does at a glance.

### The numbers
- Under 3 minutes from data to all 57 cards in the game.
- The slow part is choosing the shot, about a minute and a half per technique.
