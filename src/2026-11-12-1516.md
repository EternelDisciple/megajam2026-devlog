# Day 1, afternoon: the first act runs

![Same combat frame: the ink-and-brush interface, then the new square, sans-serif one](media/2026-11-12-1516/2026-11-12_1516_ui_v2_vs_2026.gif "Same frame, old UI then new UI.")

**In short**: act I plays end to end, and the interface went plain and readable.

### Done
- Act I loop: door hub, 7 boss fights, rewards, final boss, death and restart. Two automated runs, no crash.
- The hub keeps the isometric view. The hero stands tiny before three giant doors; the next boss's emblem, its reward and its stage are carved into each door instead of written in a menu.
- A first bestiary: seven bosses, each a vice made flesh (Wrath, Shame, Hunger…), plus a final one.

![Isometric hub: a tiny hero facing three giant doors with glowing carved emblems](media/2026-11-12-1516/2026-11-12_1457_hub_portes_iso.jpg)

### Decided
- UI: square, sans-serif, contrast checked against WCAG AA and colour-blind simulations. Less charm, far more readable. Cards keep their ink and paper.
- Main hero: an ethereal knight.

### Next
- Our autopilot cleared every fight in two test runs: every boss still shares one generic AI. Giving each its own rules comes first.

**Update (Nov 14):** We removed a win count we could not trace. Cards later moved from ink and paper to crisp-pixel frames, with a material per rarity.
