# The hall of the Heart

![Before and after: the hall of doors](media/2026-11-14-1118/2026-11-14_0958_heart_hall.jpg "Playtest build.")

**In short**: between two fights, you now climb through a real hall instead of a wall floating in the void.

### What changed
- **Doors 13 m tall**: the hero is tiny at their feet.
- **Dark inside**: no more white openings. You see only black, and a glow in the colour of the boss waiting behind.
- **The Heart of the Tower** sits in the middle of the hall. A stone heart veined with moonlight: lay your hand on it to heal.
- **A camera that follows you** as you climb, so the doors reveal themselves step by step.

### What we dropped
- Text labels everywhere: names and rewards only appear when you hover a door.
