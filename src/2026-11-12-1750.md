# Day 1, evening: one hero, five passes

![The same knight across six versions, each row a pass, rows scored from 1.8 to 3.8 out of 5](media/2026-11-12-1750/2026-11-12_1750_pion_evolution.jpg "Same skeleton, same animations, same game camera. Only the shapes, values and poses change.")

**In short**: our hero, the Broken Blade, is a small 3D figure seen from far above. Five passes took him from "box of cubes" to clean.

### What each pass fixed
- Raw cubes and cylinders become shaped volumes.
- Realistic proportions vanish at 64 pixels, so head, hands and blade are pushed bigger.
- Every colour gets three tones; nothing goes darker than the floor.
- A real stance: weight on one leg, sword beside the head instead of over the face.
- The sword break reads as broken, never as a tip.

### Next
Clean is not memorable yet. Character comes from how he moves: stance, a breathing idle, a cape that follows.
