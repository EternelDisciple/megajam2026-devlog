# Water that reads as water

![The Siren's lagoon, moving](media/2026-11-15-0717/2026-11-15_0708_water_motion.gif "The Siren's lagoon, fixed camera. Playtest build.")

**In short**: water tiles used to be a flat tinted patch, easy to mistake for a marked zone. Now they look like water at a glance.

### What changed
- A deep two-tone base, pale crests drifting slowly, and foam with a bright rim wherever water meets stone.
- Each room has its own water: green scum in the Hydra's marsh, calm green in the Medusa's garden, pearly salt water in the Siren's lagoon.
- Movement, attack and boss warning tiles stay on top, exactly as before.

### How we checked it
- Motion stays on the pixel grid: no shimmer with a still camera or while rotating it.
- With reduced motion on, the water freezes.
