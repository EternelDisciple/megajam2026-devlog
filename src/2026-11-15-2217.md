# Taller cards, then back again

![Cards in hand, 5:7 format, with a keyword bubble](media/2026-11-15-2217/2026-11-15_cheminement_cartes_57_en.jpg "Final card format, 5:7. Playtest build.")

**In short**: we tried a taller card to give the text more room. The same night, we went back.

### What we thought
- Cards felt cramped, so we asked for a taller 5:8 shape everywhere.

### What we saw
- The illustration stretched.
- The frame edges looked worse.
- Our target is two lines of text, not more height.

### What we did
- Back to 5:7, enlarged by about 7 %.
- A smaller picture, more room for the text.

### Why
A rule is only a hypothesis until the card is on screen.
