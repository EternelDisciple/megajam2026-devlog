# Attacks you can read before they land

![Four Minotaur attacks, each drawn with its own shape, name and damage](media/2026-11-13-1920/2026-11-13_1819_telegraphs.jpg "Playtest build, in French.")

**In short**: a boss arms its attack one turn ahead. Until now, every warning looked the same. Now each one says what it is.

### What changed
- The warning traces the exact shape of the attack: arc, corridor, ring.
- Its name and its damage sit on the ground, guard already counted.
- Attacks too wide to dodge are marked apart, and say what saves you: a guard, or a pillar.
- Small fixes from playtests: points left this turn instead of "3/3", portraits in the turn order, the hero's reach in blue.
