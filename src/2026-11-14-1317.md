# An open question: how much pixel art?

![The Minotaur's arena: current look and a test with painted stone](media/2026-11-14-1317/2026-11-14_1245_minotaur_da_test.jpg "Left: current. Right: test, not adopted yet.")

**In short**: we are not sure yet, and we are testing it in the game rather than guessing.

### The question
- Today, almost everything goes through our pixel filter: heroes, bosses, but also walls, floor and props.
- Big flat surfaces lose their relief, and they shimmer a little when the camera moves.

### What we tested
- Keep pixels only for the interface and the characters; paint the rest as clean stone.
- The walls clearly gained. The floor, not really.
- But the boss busts and the Heart lost their colour, and the whole felt less coherent.

### What we are trying next
- Painted stone for walls and floor only; every object stays in pixels.
- And a very light pixel filter over the painted stone, to keep one style.
