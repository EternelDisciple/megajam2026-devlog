# Day 1, night: the knight starts acting

![The Broken Blade, front and back, sweeping up his ghost sword in a wide theatrical arc](media/2026-11-12-1917/2026-11-12_1917_knight_cast.gif "Front and back views, at game scale.")

**In short**: for the first time today, our hero feels like someone.

### What changed
- Every move is now a sequence of held poses, not motion data: big anticipation, a two-frame strike, a heavy recovery.
- The sword follows real longsword technique: diagonal cuts, edge first, no impossible wrists.
- Wider stance, bent knees, feet that stay planted.
- Cape and scarf finally move, a beat behind the body.
