# Fenrir cut his chains

![Fenrir, the chained wolf boss, in the early version](media/2026-11-15-2317/2026-11-12_2300_boss_fenrir.gif "The early, chained Fenrir. Work in progress.")

**In short**: on day 1 Fenrir was chained, ate your cards and grew. On day 3 he is free.

### What we thought
- A beast tied to a stake, growing as the fight drags on.

### What we did
- No chain, no stake: he runs five tiles a turn.
- His strength is reach. His weaknesses are fire and pushes, shown in his frame.
- Against an obstacle he takes a collision and is stunned for a turn.

### Why
The chains were not fun to play against, and they made him hard to balance. A free Fenrir asks you to read his reach and use his weaknesses, and his numbers are easier to tune.
