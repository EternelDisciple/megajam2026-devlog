# Day 1 morning: a shelved idea, reopened

![The same player turn played twice: default speed on top, x2 speed below](media/2026-11-12-matin/2026-11-12_turn_compare_en.gif "Rhythm mock-up, not the final look: one turn at x1, then at x2.")

**Director's Cut**: Ascension is a game I pitched years ago, back in my student days. It was not selected. A short Unity prototype followed, then stopped for lack of time. Today it comes out of the drawer, rebuilt from scratch in Unreal. Only the idea returns.

### The core
- Tactical deckbuilder on a grid, boss fights only.
- Two decks: movement (drawn every turn, guaranteed) and action.
- Bosses are rules, not HP bags: they answer your combos or break your draw.
- Boss attacks are telegraphed a turn ahead: read, then dodge.

### Decided today
- Bigger board with obstacles, plus a turn timeline (some bosses act twice).
- Collectible cards in four rarities, each an evolution of the same power.
- Two heroes: a monk (melee) and an archer (range).
- Rhythm is a pillar: x2 speed by default.

Tooling made before the jam: [Prepared before the jam](prepared/).

**Update (Nov 14):** We dropped the monk the same day; the hero is now the Broken Blade, a knight, and a second hero is out of scope. The two decks were replaced on Nov 13 by one deck plus action and movement points.
