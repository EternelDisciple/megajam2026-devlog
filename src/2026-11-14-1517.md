# Answer to our open question: the busts

![Door busts: stone gargoyles, or the boss itself in pixels](media/2026-11-14-1517/2026-11-14_1403_door_busts.jpg "Top: stone. Middle: kept. Playtest build.")

**In short**: part of our pixel-art question is settled. Above each door, the boss bust is now the boss itself.

### What we tried
- Grey stone gargoyles, carved in the same painted stone as the walls.
- They looked clean, but from a distance nobody read them as "the Minotaur" or "the Hydra".

### What we kept
- The bust uses the same model, colours and pixel treatment as the boss you will fight.
- It sits in a stone niche, lit by the boss's own glow.
- Rule we are now testing everywhere: objects and characters in crisp pixels, big surfaces calmer behind them.

### Still open
- How to pixelate the walls and floor without them shimmering when the camera moves.
