# Day 1, early morning: from grey box to ink

![Side by side: the animated rhythm mock-up on the left, a turn played in the engine on the right](media/2026-11-12-0815/2026-11-12_0810_maquette_vs_jeu.gif "Same turn read, mock-up on the left, engine on the right.")

**In short**: the real game now reads like this morning's mock-up. Paper and ink, blue for the hero, red for the boss's threat.

### Done
- 16×16 board with corridors: the hero slips through, the 2×2 boss cannot.
- Cards rebuilt the Slay the Spire way: rules text on the face, the area opens on hover, in a pop-up and on the board.
- One sorted hand: movement on the left, actions on the right.
- A performance baseline, measured and tracked: about 390 FPS on the dev machine.

### Decided today
- Energy per turn: 4.
- Small steps are free, big moves cost energy. Every turn becomes a choice: dodge or strike.

**Update (Nov 14):** Energy 4 was a test, replaced on Nov 13 by action and movement points. The 2×2 boss has left the run. We removed a hitch figure we could not trace.
