# Twenty-two relics, twenty-two objects

![The 22 relics, each modelled as a small object](media/2026-11-14-0118/2026-11-13_2133_relics.jpg "Simulated game render.")

**In short**: relics change how you play. They deserved more than flat icons, so each one became an object.

### What we did
- Every relic is a small sculpted object: a cracked urn, an hourglass caught in ice, a stone worn smooth by hands, a split anvil.
- Same sculpted language as the door statues and our two allies.
- One glowing part per object, tinted by its path: fire, ice, stone, neutral.
- The icons in the interface are renders of these objects, crisp pixels, no outline.
