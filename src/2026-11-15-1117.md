# The Siren doesn't die the first time

![The Siren melts and rises again](media/2026-11-15-1117/2026-11-15_1042_siren_puddle.gif "The Siren melts into a puddle, flows to the fountain and rises with her water double. Playtest build.")

**In short**: when you first bring the final boss down, she melts into water instead of falling.

### What happens
- The puddle slides across the floor, around a column, leaving a trail that dries behind it.
- It pours into the fountain in the middle of the room. The fountain boils.
- The Siren climbs back out, possessed, and her double made of water rises on the other side of the basin.

### Why a fountain in the middle
- Her room used to keep the water on the edges. Putting the fountain at the center makes it the heart of the fight: everything she does goes through it.
- The room was widened so the fight still has space around it.
