# Reading your deck at a glance

![The new deck view in combat](media/2026-11-15-0117/2026-11-14_2331_deck_view.png "Deck view, 19 cards. Playtest build.")

**In short**: the deck view was a flat list. Now it answers one question fast: what can I do with this deck?

### How it reads
- One row per role: Movement, Technique, Defense, Attack, with a count.
- Inside each row, cards are sorted by element (Neutral, Fire, Ice, Stone), then by cost.
- Duplicates stack into one card marked x2.
- No filters, no toggles: one layout to learn.

### Where it opens
- The same view in combat, in the hub and on every pick screen.
- Hover a card to enlarge it with its keyword bubbles; wheel to scroll, right click or Esc to close.
