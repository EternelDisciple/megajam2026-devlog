# From a skill to its card, in one loop

![One fire skill at four power levels, each with its own animation and effect](media/2026-11-13-0829/2026-11-13_0814_fire_niagara_en.mp4 "Work in progress: one fire skill, four levels.")

**In short**: one fire skill, four power levels, and the card art comes from the same scene.

### What it does
- Each level changes the animation: one strike, two, a ground slam, then the final technique.
- The pattern on the grid grows with the level, and the effect draws exactly that pattern.
- The hero's broken blade takes on the element of the attack.
- The same staging serves the game and the card illustration: we capture the moment of impact in the engine.
- We checked the result in hand, at real size.

### Decision
The loop works. We validate it on fire only before doing the other elements.

The effect style is a first draft. It is the next job.
