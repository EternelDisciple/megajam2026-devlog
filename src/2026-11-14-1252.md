# The Siren's song, without words

![The Siren's song, rewritten for orchestra](media/2026-11-14-1252/2026-11-14_1238_siren_score.png "Every bar is a note of the final score.")

**In short**: the final boss now has her theme, hummed, then rewritten note by note for a small orchestra.

### What we tried, and doubted
- Lyrics in French, in alexandrines: too stiff to sing.
- Lyrics in the knight's voice, then in English: better, but words kept pulling the song toward a pop ballad.
- We hesitated for a while, then dropped the words. A hummed voice says "sorrow" without explaining it.

### What we kept
- One hummed melody we liked, with its long held notes and a pause before the chorus.
- The same melody, played by harp, strings and flute, with her voice on top.
- The menu loops the opening verses, so you may recognise the tune when you finally meet her.
