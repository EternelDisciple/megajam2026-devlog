# Game feel: the boss now takes a breath

![Boss turn before and after: the new version telegraphs the attack and hits with more weight](media/2026-11-13-0542/2026-11-13_0144_tour_avant_apres_en.mp4 "Before / after, same boss, same speed (two separate autoplayed turns). Work in progress.")

**In short**: we tuned timing, measured, and kept what reads.

### Changed
- The boss pauses before acting, so the threat is readable even at double speed.
- Impact freeze follows the strength of the hit: light, heavy, critical.
- The scenery answers impacts: dust, embers, shockwaves.
- The hub and rooms feel alive: floating embers, and the boss follows the hero with its eyes.

### Why
A flat freeze made a light bite feel as heavy as a finishing blow. Short waits hid the telegraph. Weight and warning matter more than speed.

**Update (Nov 14):** The two clips compare pacing, not the same moves.
