# Why the arena looked like pixel mush

![One turn in the rebuilt Minotaur arena](media/2026-11-14-1917/2026-11-14_1836_minotaur_turn.gif "Rebuilt Minotaur arena. Playtest build.")

**In short**: the pixel filter was fine. What we fed it was not.

### What was wrong
- Walls, floor and background shared the same blue value.
- Bricks and cracks were smaller than one art pixel, so they turned into noise.
- Generic pack props: grey boxes that told no story.
- Flat light, and an opaque telegraph covering the board.

### What we did
- Clear value planes: near-black walls, muted floor, bright characters.
- Every detail at least two art pixels wide.
- Props modelled for this room only: horned braziers, bull skulls, a portcullis arch.
- A cold moon shaft, warm pools of firelight, darkness beyond the walls.

### Next
- This room becomes the template for the other arenas.
