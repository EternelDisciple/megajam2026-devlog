# We planned a monk. We shipped a knight.

![The hero select screen: the Knight, then three locked slots, the first one the Archer](media/2026-11-15-2117/2026-11-15_0407_choix_heros_en.jpg "Hero select. Playtest build.")

**In short**: our two planned heroes were a monk and an archer. The monk is gone, the knight leads, and the archer waits behind a lock.

### What changed
- The monk was dropped on day 1: his look never convinced us.
- The knight became the hero. He was "The Ethereal Knight", but we could not make him look ethereal in time: he looks like a knight, so now he is "The Knight".
- The archer stays as a locked preview, with a one-line promise.

### Hero choice moved
It used to be its own screen, with the knight already standing in the scene behind. Now you choose from the main menu, then the run starts.
