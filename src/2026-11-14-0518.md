# One room sets the bar

![The Minotaur's hall, before and after](media/2026-11-14-0518/2026-11-14_0455_hall_before_after.jpg "Playtest build.")

**In short**: we rebuilt the Minotaur's hall as the reference room for the whole game.

### The light
- A cold moon as the key light.
- Embers kept to a few small warm spots.
- The walls fall into a dark you can still read.

### The stone
- Flat palette tones with banded shading, the same treatment as our objects.
- The pillars no longer turn black.

### What you can touch
- Gold, embers, a healing cup and souls now sit on the board as small sculpted objects.
- Each one casts a soft pool of colour on the floor, so the light shows what you can pick up.
- Some blocks can be pushed: shove one into the bull's lane for cover, or ram it into him.
