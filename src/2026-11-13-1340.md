# Dying is part of the climb

![The hero falls down the tower: no game over screen](media/2026-11-13-1340/2026-11-13_1329_fall.jpg "Playtest build, in French.")

**In short**: in a roguelike you die a lot. We made death fast, and part of the journey.

### What it does
- No game over screen. The hero tumbles down the tower, like Sisyphus's rock.
- One line sums up the run during the fall.
- About 1.4 seconds from the fatal blow to "Climb again".
- An ethereal ally greets you: "Ah, there you are again."

### Decisions
- The ally replaces our starting choice. It hands you five cards at once: keep some, all, or none.
- Every screen in the loop stays short. No dead time between two attempts.

![The ally's first cards at the time: pick what you keep (since replaced by one out of three)](media/2026-11-13-1340/2026-11-13_1329_booster.jpg "Playtest build, in French.")
